Slide Types

Seven layouts

The skill infers layout from your markdown structure. H1 alone becomes hero. H2 with bullets becomes content. No configuration needed.

  • hero — full-bleed opening or closing
  • section-divider — major section transition
  • content — heading + body + bullets
  • cards — 2 to 4 items in a grid
  • split — two-column comparison
  • diagram — flow with nodes and arrows
  • quote — centered pull quote
