Slide Types

Seven layouts, auto-inferred

The skill reads your markdown structure and picks the right layout. You can also be explicit with fenced blocks.

  • hero — full-bleed opening or closing slide
  • section-divider — transition between major parts
  • content — heading + body text + bullets
  • cards — 2–4 parallel items in a grid
  • split — two-column before/after or comparison
  • diagram — flow chart with nodes and arrows
  • quote — centered pull quote
