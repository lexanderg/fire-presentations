Slide Types

Seven layouts, auto-inferred

The skill reads your markdown structure and selects the right layout. Or use fenced blocks for full control.

  • hero — full-bleed opening or closing slide
  • section-divider — transition between major sections
  • content — heading, subheading, and bullet list
  • cards — 2 to 4 parallel items in a grid
  • split — two-column before/after or comparison
  • diagram — flow chart with nodes and arrows
  • quote — centered pull quote with attribution
